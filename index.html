    Why this matters

    Today every new browserCoin node gets introduced to the others through two small servers I run. If they go down, nobody new can join, and a network nobody can join slowly dies.

    This test shows the fix: the introduction is just a short code, and any channel can carry it. A DM, a QR code, a photo of a screen. After that the two browsers talk directly, encrypted. The next browserCoin release uses this exact code.
